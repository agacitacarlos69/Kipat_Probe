# Graphite Precision

> **The card is defined by one precision orbit around the contact point.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Character | Serious, technical, quiet, premium. |
| Tone | Dark |
| Material | Soft-touch laminated white-core PVC with a graphite flood (fallback: matte laminate). |
| Finish | Raised spot gloss on the orbit (1.0 mm band over a 0.35 mm printed tonal line) and a gloss contact point. Feel the orbit with a thumb. |
| Inks | Graphite flood, graphite-line tone, soft white (lockup, satellite), cobalt-on-dark (contact point). |
| Production complexity | MEDIUM: soft-touch PVC (vendor-dependent) plus one raised gloss plate. |
| Reveal pairing | Precision Orbit (recommended, not locked; the owner may choose any Reveal). |

## Front wireframe (before art)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ (•)) kipat                                   │  lockup: says Kipat once, quietly
│                                  ·  ╭───╮ ∘  │  satellite: makes it an orbit, not a circle
│                                   ╭╯     ╰╮  │
│                                   │   ●   │  │  contact point: where to tap
│                                   ╰╮     ╭╯  │  orbit (r 15 mm): the one idea
│                                     ╰───╯    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Element | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| Lockup | Recognition without branding the person's object; top-left on every edition. |
| Orbit, r 15 mm, 0.35 mm tonal line | The single idea. A tonal line (not white) keeps it quiet in daylight; the raised gloss makes it tactile. Offset to the 3/4 line so the card is asymmetric and intentional. |
| Satellite, Ø 1.1 mm at −38° | Turns a circle into an orbit (motion implied, as in the Reveal's arc). Without it the ring reads as a generic target. |
| Contact point | Kipat's shared signature; tells you where to tap without an NFC icon. |

Nothing else is on the front.

## Back (shared Kipat architecture)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Vash Puno                                    │  name: who this card belongs to
│ BS Computer Science                          │  context: why you'd remember them
│ Polytechnic University of the Philippines    │
│                                     ┌──────┐ │
│                                     │  QR  │ │  fallback for phones without NFC/Kipat (optional)
│ (•)) Tap to connect                 └──────┘ │  the one instruction; the mark is the tap cue
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Identical on every edition, so any Kipat card is recognisable from the back. The generic inventory
back replaces the name block with the lockup and prints no QR (`back-generic.svg`).

## Critique

- *Risk: black card with a blue dot.* The orbit is tonal, the cobalt is 2.4 mm and nothing glows, so
  it doesn't read as "black card with blue neon". Keep the orbit tonal; never brighten it to white.
- *Risk: bank card.* There is no chip rectangle on the left, no embossing and no corner network mark,
  and the motif sits on the right. Passes.
- Without finishes it is still the orbit (printed). It photographs well even badly, because of the high contrast.
