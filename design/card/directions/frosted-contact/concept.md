# Frosted Contact

> **Touch leaves a mark: two soft rings spread from one contact point in frosted PVC.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Character | Human, modern, light, tactile. |
| Tone | Material-led |
| Material | Frosted translucent PVC (clear core, frosted both faces). Nothing printed as a flood; the stock is the colour. |
| Finish | White ink is the finish: opaque white underbase under every coloured element; the two rings are white ink only (milky lines in the frost). |
| Inks | White ink, graphite, cobalt. QR on a white-ink patch. |
| Production complexity | MEDIUM–HIGH: special stock plus a white-ink plate; antenna may be visible through the frost (decide with a sample). |
| Reveal pairing | Contact Ripple (recommended, not locked). |

## Front wireframe (before art)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ (•)) kipat                                   │  lockup on white underbase: legible on frost
│                                 ◌ ◌ ◌ ◌      │
│                               ◌   ○ ○   ◌    │  inner ring r 5.5, outer r 10.5 (white ink)
│                               ◌  ○ ● ○  ◌    │  contact point (cobalt on white)
│                               ◌   ○ ○   ◌    │
│                                 ◌ ◌ ◌ ◌      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Element | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| Lockup | As on every edition; printed over white so thin strokes don't vanish in the frost. |
| Two white-ink rings | The single idea: a touch spreading outwards, the physical version of the Contact Ripple. Only two, so it's a ripple and not a target or waves pattern. |
| Contact point | The origin of the ripple and the tap cue. |
| No flood | Material honesty: the translucency *is* the design; a printed flood would make it an ordinary card. |

Nothing else is on the front.

## Back (shared Kipat architecture)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Vash Puno                                    │  name: who this card belongs to
│ BS Computer Science                          │  context: why you'd remember them
│ Polytechnic University of the Philippines    │
│                                     ┌──────┐ │
│                                     │  QR  │ │  fallback for phones without NFC/Kipat (optional)
│ (•)) Tap to connect                 └──────┘ │  the one instruction; the mark is the tap cue
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Identical on every edition, so any Kipat card is recognisable from the back. The generic inventory
back replaces the name block with the lockup and prints no QR (`back-generic.svg`).

## Critique

- *Risk: transparent gaming or employee card.* Frosted, not clear; no full-colour print; two quiet rings.
  Avoid clear stock. Avoid showing the antenna unless a sample proves it looks deliberate.
- *Risk: legibility.* Every glyph has a white underbase; the back QR sits on a white patch.
- The proof *simulates* translucency (a grey strip passes under the card). A real stock sample is required before approval.
