# Aperture

> **One flat aperture of six segments opens onto the contact point.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Character | Recognisable, geometric, focused. |
| Tone | Neutral |
| Material | Matte laminated white-core PVC with a stone flood. |
| Finish | Spot gloss (flat) on the six segments: matte stone around gloss graphite. |
| Inks | Stone flood, graphite (segments, text), cobalt. |
| Production complexity | LOW–MEDIUM: standard PVC plus one flat gloss plate. |
| Reveal pairing | Aperture (recommended, not locked). |

## Front wireframe (before art)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ (•)) kipat                                   │  lockup
│                                   ▄▄▄▄▄      │
│                                 ▟▛ ╱ ▜▙      │  six flat segments, slanted cuts (24°)
│                                 █ ( ● ) █    │  round opening r 5.2: the point is revealed
│                                 ▜▙ ╱ ▟▛      │  outer r 11
│                                   ▀▀▀▀▀      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Element | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| Aperture, six flat segments | The single idea: something opens to reveal a person, like the Reveal itself. Six is the fewest that reads as an aperture rather than a pie chart. |
| Round opening, gentle 24° cuts | A first draft with a hexagonal opening and a 32° twist read as a literal camera shutter and was rejected. Round and gentle reads as an aperture *mark*. |
| Contact point in the opening | Gives the aperture a reason: it opens onto the tap. |
| Stone base | The neutral edition: neither black nor white. |

Nothing else is on the front.

## Back (shared Kipat architecture)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Vash Puno                                    │  name: who this card belongs to
│ BS Computer Science                          │  context: why you'd remember them
│ Polytechnic University of the Philippines    │
│                                     ┌──────┐ │
│                                     │  QR  │ │  fallback for phones without NFC/Kipat (optional)
│ (•)) Tap to connect                 └──────┘ │  the one instruction; the mark is the tap cue
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Identical on every edition, so any Kipat card is recognisable from the back. The generic inventory
back replaces the name block with the lockup and prints no QR (`back-generic.svg`).

## Critique

- *Risk: camera brand or photo app.* Mitigated by flat segments, no chrome, no lens glass and no shadow.
  Keep a reviewer asking "is this a camera icon?" at every change.
- The most iconic silhouette of the six, recognisable at a glance across a table.
