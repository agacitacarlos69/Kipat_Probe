Create an extremely simple thin digital identity slab for motion reference.

It is NOT a real credit card. NOT a bank card. NOT a business card mockup. It is an abstract UI surface.

Geometry: very thin rounded rectangular plane, subtle 12–16dp-equivalent corner character, flat front, very shallow thickness, no embossed details, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no texture.

Material: matte graphite or soft neutral polymer, extremely restrained surface response, no metallic finish, no iridescence, no holographic effect.

The goal is to study: edge thickness, small perspective movement, light falloff, subtle physicality — not decoration.

Create a neutral orthographic/near-orthographic presentation.

If animation is supported: start nearly edge-on, rotate only 3–5 degrees toward viewer, settle completely flat.

No spinning. No floating. No camera orbit. No dramatic lighting. No environment.

Transparent background or neutral isolated background.
