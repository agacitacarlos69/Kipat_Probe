## Notes per direction

**A — Quiet Graphite.** Best on every task and platform criterion. Its weakness is
distinctiveness: clean neutral UIs converge. Mitigation is built into the plan — the identity is
set as type, not boxed; the only card in the shell previews the actual recipient card; and the
Reveal carries the brand moment (contact ring + cobalt edge trace).

**B — Editorial Identity.** Most "human" Home, but: warm neutral + big display type drifts toward
the most common AI-generated look; editorial devices (em-dash index lines, text-arrow CTAs) are
themselves tells; low density hurts Connections; a text-styled Share hides the main job; a quiet
fade loses the contact moment in the Reveal.

**C — Technical Utility.** Fast for power users, but identity becomes one dense row (fails
"identity is the hero"), icon-only share methods hurt first-time discoverability, and a generic
bottom-sheet Reveal erases Kipat's defining interaction.

## Decision

- **Primary: A — Quiet Graphite.**
- **Backup: B — Editorial Identity**, specifically for its identity typesetting. One element is
  borrowed into A: the identity block sits on the page (no container) with generous space above
  and below, so it reads as "this is me", not "this is a widget".

Chosen on task and trust criteria, not visual excitement.
